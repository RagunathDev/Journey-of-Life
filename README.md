# 🌱 Journey of Life

**Journey of Life** is a symbolic, level-based web experience that represents the stages of a man’s life — from education, to responsibility, to legacy.

Each level reflects growth, purpose, and progression through simple visuals and meaningful metaphors.

🔗 **Live Demo**  
👉 https://ragunathdev.github.io/Journey-of-Life/

---

## 🎯 Concept

Life is not just about moving forward —  
it’s about **evolving roles, responsibilities, and priorities**.

This project visualizes that evolution through **three distinct levels**, each representing a major phase of life.

---

## 🧠 Levels Explained

### 🧑‍🎓 Level 1 — *The Scholar*
> *“Pushing the wheel of knowledge to graduate.”*

- Represents the **learning phase**
- Focused on education, discipline, and effort
- The character progresses by pushing **knowledge itself**

**Theme:** Growth through learning

---

### 🚲 Level 2 — *The Provider*
> *“Riding a bike with his wife, collecting coins to build a future.”*

- Represents early **responsibility and partnership**
- Life becomes about earning, supporting, and planning
- Coins symbolize **financial stability and effort**

**Theme:** Responsibility and partnership

---

### 🚗 Level 3 — *The Legacy*
> *“Driving a car with the whole family, collecting wealth to secure the legacy.”*

- Represents **stability, family, and long-term vision**
- Wealth is no longer for survival, but for **security and legacy**
- The journey now includes the **next generation**

**Theme:** Stability and legacy building
